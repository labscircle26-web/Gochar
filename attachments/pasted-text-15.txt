In the Upcoming Celestial Events popup, add a distinctive animated golden highlight to every event card where a planet enters a different zodiac sign.

Examples:

- Mars enters Leo
- Jupiter enters Cancer
- Saturn enters Pisces
- Venus enters Libra

Animation design

- Add a subtle animated champagne-gold border around the entire event card.
- Use a gentle golden glow that softly pulses.
- Add a very subtle moving golden shimmer along the border.
- Keep the animation elegant and premium, matching the existing dark celestial and gold theme.
- Do not make the animation too bright, distracting or fast.

Important conditions

1. Apply this animation ONLY to planetary sign-entry events.
2. Do not animate Nakshatra changes.
3. Do not animate Pada changes.
4. Do not animate conjunctions.
5. Do not animate Drishti events.
6. Do not animate retrograde or combustion events.

Dynamic behaviour

- Detect sign-entry events using the actual event type/data, not text matching alone.
- Apply the animation automatically to every qualifying planet.
- It must work regardless of which Graha is selected.
- Preserve existing event calculations, sorting, countdowns and card layouts.

The golden animation should immediately help users identify major zodiac sign transitions among all upcoming celestial events.
