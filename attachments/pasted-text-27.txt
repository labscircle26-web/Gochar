GOCHAR — FINAL USER-FACING RESPONSE STYLE

Update the prediction output system so users receive only the final, complete astrological interpretation.

Do not expose internal processing or analysis to users.

Requirements:

1. Never display what books, PDFs, reference files or knowledge sources the system is currently reading.
2. Never show internal reasoning, rule matching, retrieval steps, calculation procedures or backend processing.
3. Do not display technical explanations of how the AI arrived at its prediction.
4. Do not show intermediate interpretations from individual books separately.
5. Do not show internal confidence calculations, scoring systems or diagnostic information.
6. Do not produce responses such as "I am checking your 10th lord" or "I found three matching rules."
7. Combine all relevant astrological information into one coherent, polished prediction.
8. Avoid repeating the same planetary interpretation in multiple sections.
9. Present the conclusion naturally, as if an experienced Vedic astrologer is explaining the person's chart.
10. Keep technical astrological terminology where useful, but explain its meaning in ordinary language.

FINAL OUTPUT FORMAT

When a user taps a house, provide:

- Overall prediction.
- Detailed interpretation.
- Positive indications.
- Challenges and possible obstacles.
- Practical life outcomes.
- Relevant timing and upcoming periods.
- Final conclusion.

Include classical book references in a discreet References section at the bottom, only when appropriate or requested.

IMPORTANT

The system must still perform all necessary calculations, research, source verification and multi-layer analysis internally.

This instruction changes only what the user sees, not the depth or quality of the underlying analysis.

The user should receive one compiled, complete, personalized answer — never a report of how the AI is working behind the scenes.

Apply this consistently to all houses, planets, yogas, dashas, transits and prediction modules throughout Gochar.
