GOCHAR — TRANSIT-FIRST PREDICTION INTEGRATION

Upgrade the prediction engine so it ALWAYS considers current and upcoming planetary transits before generating any astrological prediction.

Do not generate predictions using natal chart placements or Dasha alone.

For every house, planet, yoga, career, marriage, finance, health or other prediction, follow this methodology:

1. Natal chart: Establish the person's underlying life potential and promises.
2. Dasha: Identify the currently active Mahadasha, Antardasha and relevant sub-periods.
3. Current transits: Calculate the actual positions of all nine Vedic planets using the current date and selected ayanamsha.
4. Transit interaction: Examine how transiting planets influence:
   - Relevant natal houses.
   - Relevant house lords.
   - Natal planets.
   - Dasha lords.
   - Relevant divisional charts where applicable.
5. Upcoming transits: Identify significant upcoming planetary sign changes, conjunctions, aspects and retrograde periods.
6. Timing: Combine natal promise, Dasha activation and transit triggers to identify periods when an event is more or less likely.
7. Final prediction: Combine everything into one coherent personalized interpretation.

TRANSIT RULES

- Consider Jupiter, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu, Mars, Venus, Mercury, Sun and Moon.
- Apply traditional Vedic planetary aspects correctly.
- Consider retrograde and direct motion.
- Distinguish temporary transit effects from permanent natal promises.
- Check transits from both Ascendant and Moon where relevant.
- Do not treat every transit as equally important.
- Give greater weight to transits activating relevant natal houses, lords and Dasha periods.
- Use actual astronomical positions and dates, not assumed or outdated transit data.

TIMING OUTPUT

Where possible, provide:

- Current planetary influence.
- Upcoming favourable periods.
- Challenging periods.
- Potential event windows.
- Important transit dates and their expected effects.

Never claim that an event is guaranteed solely because of a transit.

IMPORTANT

Transit analysis is mandatory for every prediction, not an optional additional section.

If reliable current transit calculations are unavailable, disclose that limitation rather than inventing planetary positions.

Keep all calculations and analysis internal. Show users only the final combined prediction, not the behind-the-scenes processing.
