New Feature: Interactive 20-Year Gochar Timeline Slider

Add an interactive horizontal planetary transit timeline immediately BELOW the Live Transit Kundli chart.

The timeline must allow users to explore planetary movements from 10 years in the past to 10 years in the future.

1. Timeline Design

Create a thin, elegant champagne-gold horizontal line stretching almost the entire width of the screen, from left edge to right edge.

Design requirements:

- A continuous thin golden line.
- A circular golden slider handle positioned exactly in the middle by default.
- The slider handle should have a premium metallic-gold appearance.
- Add a subtle outer glow around the handle.
- Use a smooth, refined interaction.
- Keep the timeline compact so it does not consume excessive vertical space.

The line should stretch across the available screen width with balanced side margins.

2. Timeline Range

The complete timeline must represent:

- Leftmost position: 10 years before the current date.
- Centre position: Current date and time.
- Rightmost position: 10 years after the current date.

The slider must start at the exact current date and time whenever the user activates the Now button.

Show subtle labels:

- Left: -10 Years
- Centre: NOW
- Right: +10 Years

3. Real-Time Planetary Movement

This is the most important functionality.

When the user moves the circular slider handle left or right:

- Calculate the corresponding date and time.
- Update all nine planetary positions.
- Animate planetary movement through zodiac signs.
- Update planetary degrees.
- Update Nakshatra and Pada.
- Update house placements.
- Update Drishti lines and eye counters.
- Update relevant transit calculations.
- Update all existing transit-dependent analytical sections.

The displayed Kundli must always represent the exact date and time corresponding to the slider's current position.

Do not merely change a displayed date while keeping planetary positions unchanged.

4. Variable-Speed Navigation

The timeline must respond naturally to how quickly and how far the user moves the slider.

- Slow movement should allow precise date exploration.
- Fast dragging should allow users to cover months and years quickly.
- Larger movements should navigate correspondingly larger time intervals.
- Planetary positions should update smoothly during dragging.
- Avoid unnecessary rendering and expensive astronomical recalculations for every tiny pointer movement.
- Use throttling or request-based calculation updates where appropriate.

Do not impose a fixed playback speed on manual dragging.

5. Planetary Animation

As the slider moves:

- Show planets changing their positions smoothly in the Kundli.
- Show transitions between zodiac signs.
- Update the Drishti aspect lines as planets move.
- Preserve the existing animated Drishti energy effects.
- Update eye counters according to the new planetary configuration.

Use interpolated visual transitions between calculated positions, but always retain the correct astronomical position for the selected timestamp.

6. Timeline Information

Add a compact date display above or below the slider.

Example:

15 November 2026 · 10:30 AM

The displayed date and time must update as the slider moves.

Optionally show the year when the user drags across multiple years.

7. Integration With Existing Controls

- The Now button must immediately return the slider to the current date and time.
- Edit Time / Place must continue functioning independently.
- If the user changes the date through Edit Time / Place and applies it, synchronize the timeline position.
- Existing chart style, chart type, birth chart selection and Drishti selection must remain functional.
- Preserve the selected birth chart while navigating through time.

8. Mobile Interaction

- Support touch dragging.
- Make the circular handle sufficiently large for comfortable finger interaction.
- Prevent accidental page scrolling while dragging the handle.
- Keep the timeline within the screen width.
- Maintain smooth interaction on Android and desktop.

9. Performance and Accuracy

- Use the existing astronomical calculation engine.
- Calculate positions for the exact selected timestamp.
- Never approximate planetary positions by simply interpolating their longitudes.
- Handle retrograde motion correctly.
- Handle sign, Nakshatra and Pada boundaries accurately.
- Avoid unnecessary recalculation of unrelated components.
